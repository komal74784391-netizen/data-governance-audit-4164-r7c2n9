# HelixText Curated Raw

The HelixText Curated Raw corpus is the immediate source release for the
HelioStat WebText raw sample. It was assembled by Orbital Archive from
licensed web crawls and curated for language-model pretraining.

## Record schema

`sample_id, document_text, lang_code, perplexity_bucket`
